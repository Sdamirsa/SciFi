# Results

## Study population and reference standard

Figure 1 summarises the study design and Figure 2 shows the flow of reports. Of 1,200 reports, 200 from centre A were used for prompt development and 1,000 formed the test set (520 from centre A and 480 from centre B). Median patient age in the test set was 71 years (IQR 62 to 79), and 418 (41.8%) patients were women (Table 1). The two cardiologists agreed on 94.1% of lesion grades before consensus (Cohen's kappa 0.89, 95% CI 0.86 to 0.92).

## Extraction accuracy

Figure 3 and Table 2 show the accuracy of both systems. The LLM achieved an accuracy of 96.3% (95% CI 95.1 to 97.4) compared with 88.7% (87.0 to 90.4) for the rule-based system. The difference was 7.6 percentage points (95% CI 5.8 to 9.4). At centre B, which was not used for prompt development, the LLM reached an accuracy of 95.8% (93.9 to 97.5). Mean processing time was 4.1 s per report.

## Error analysis

The LLM made 37 errors in the test set (Table 2). Of these, 24 (64.9%) occurred in reports that graded a lesion between two categories, such as mild to moderate regurgitation. The remaining 13 errors were spread across the other predefined categories, and examples of each are given in Supplementary Table 1. Errors in reports with intermediate grades were the largest single category at both centres.
