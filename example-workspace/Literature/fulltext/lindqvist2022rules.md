# A rule-based system for extracting valve lesion severity from echocardiography reports

Lindqvist E, Moreau C. Fictional Informatics in Medicine 2022. doi:10.0000/scifi.example.003 (fictional)

## Abstract

We built a rule-based system of 412 regular expressions that extracts the severity of four valve lesions from echocardiography reports and evaluated it on 1,500 reports from the hospital where it was written. Precision for any graded lesion was 97.1% (95% CI 96.3 to 97.8). Accuracy fell to 81.4% (77.2 to 85.1) on the subset of reports that described a lesion between two grades.

## Methods

Two informaticians wrote the rules on 300 reports from 2019 and froze them before testing on 1,500 reports from 2020. Two cardiologists labelled the test reports with consensus adjudication. Reports describing an intermediate grade (for example "mild to moderate") were analysed as a predefined subgroup.

## Results

Precision was 97.1% (96.3 to 97.8) and recall was 90.6% (89.4 to 91.7). Of 1,500 reports, 362 contained an intermediate grade, and accuracy in this subgroup was 81.4% (77.2 to 85.1). The system was not tested at another hospital.
