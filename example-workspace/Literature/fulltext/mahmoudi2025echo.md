# A proprietary large language model for grading valvular heart disease in echocardiography reports: a single-centre evaluation

Mahmoudi S, Keller J, Fischer A. Journal of Invented Clinical Informatics 2025. doi:10.0000/scifi.example.005 (fictional)

## Abstract

We evaluated a proprietary large language model, accessed through a commercial cloud service, for grading the severity of aortic, mitral and tricuspid lesions from 640 transthoracic echocardiography reports at one university hospital. One cardiologist labelled every report. The model reached an accuracy of 93.5% (95% CI 92.1 to 94.8) compared with 86.2% (84.4 to 87.9) for the hospital's rule-based extraction system. Reports were sent to the external service after removal of direct identifiers under a data processing agreement.

## Methods

Reports written in English between 2021 and 2022 were included consecutively. A single cardiologist graded each lesion as none, mild, moderate or severe from the report text. The model received a fixed prompt with grading definitions and returned one grade per lesion. No external centre was included, and the prompt was developed and tested on reports from the same hospital.

## Results

Of 640 reports, 600 were analysed after exclusion of 40 without a valve assessment. Accuracy was 93.5% (95% CI 92.1 to 94.8) for the model and 86.2% (84.4 to 87.9) for the rule-based system. Most model errors involved tricuspid regurgitation graded as trivial, which the label set did not include.
