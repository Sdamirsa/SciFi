# Sources

The 14 papers the skill was learned from, what each contributed, and the weaknesses that were deliberately not copied. Two passes were made per paper: prose patterns (global and per section) and figures, tables, captions and organisation. Where the published version was unreachable the preprint of the same paper was used, so back-matter and typesetting details for those are approximate. The references describe patterns; no text from the papers is reproduced.

## Corpus-wide counts that shaped the corrective rules

- Confidence intervals for headline metrics: present in 6 of 14, and in only 3 of 14 in the running text.
- Reporting guideline named: 0 of 14 (one cited TRIPOD+AI in the Discussion only).
- Ethics statement present: 7 of 14.
- Main-text Table 1 of characteristics: 5 of 14.
- Captions passing the stand-alone test: 5 of 14.
- Figures cited in numerical order: 11 of 14.
- Dedicated limitations paragraph or section: 8 of 14.
- Promotional wording present: 12 of 14.
- Abbreviation hygiene without a defect: 0 of 14.
- Numbers that disagree between sections: 4 of 14.
