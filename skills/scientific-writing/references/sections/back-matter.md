# Back matter

The headings, their order and their standard phrasing, by journal family. Write every heading the journal lists even when the content is "none"; the corpus papers that omitted Code availability or Author contributions were in journals that require them.

## Order by journal

| Journal | Order after the main text |
|---|---|
| Nature-portfolio (Nature, Nature Cancer, Nature Communications, npj) | Methods (if last); Reporting summary statement; Data availability; Code availability; References; Acknowledgements; Author contributions; Funding (Nature) or inside Acknowledgements (npj, Nat Comms); Competing interests; Additional information |
| JAMIA | Author contributions (CRediT); Supplementary material; Funding; Conflicts of interest; Data availability; Acknowledgements; References |
| Lancet Digital Health | Contributors; Declaration of interests; Data sharing; Acknowledgements; References. "Role of the funding source" is the last Methods subsection |
| European Heart Journal | Supplementary material; Acknowledgements; Funding; Conflict of interest; (Data availability, now required); References |

## Standard content and phrasing

**Data availability.** One to three sentences. Distinguish public, controlled and unavailable data, and give the route for each.
- Public: name the repository, the accession or URL, and the licence.
- Controlled: "The [dataset] cannot be shared publicly because [patient privacy, licensing]. De-identified data are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to a data use agreement that prohibits re-identification and to approval by [committee]." Name the access process (PhysioNet credentialing for MIMIC).
- Derived resources: "The generated [benchmark, labels, risk scores] are deposited at [URL]."
- Nature-portfolio: add "Source data are provided with this paper." when figure data are supplied.
- A review with no data: "No new data were generated; all data supporting the figures are in the cited sources."
- "Available upon reasonable request" alone is the weakest acceptable form and will be queried by npj and Nature.

**Code availability.** Repository URL, licence (MIT, Apache 2.0), version or commit, and a citable DOI (Zenodo) where possible. If code or weights are withheld, give the specific reason (safety of unmonitored medical use, licensing) and what is offered instead (prompts, evaluation scripts, access via collaboration). List third-party code used.

**Author contributions.** JAMIA and Lancet accept CRediT roles by initials ("Conceptualisation: AB, CD. Methodology: ..."). Nature-portfolio and EHJ use free text by initials grouped by activity (conception and design, data curation, technical implementation, evaluation, clinical input, writing, supervision). End with "All authors reviewed and approved the manuscript" and name a guarantor who takes responsibility for the integrity of the work. Co-first authors are marked.

**Funding.** Grant numbers with the funder and the author initials they supported. Add "The funders had no role in study design, data collection and analysis, decision to publish, or preparation of the manuscript." Open access fees may be named.

**Competing interests.** Per author by initials: employment, stock, patents (with numbers), consultancies, spin-out companies. Close with "The remaining authors declare no competing interests." or "The authors declare no competing interests."

**Acknowledgements.** Named colleagues and their contribution, data providers, computing resources. In Nature-portfolio and some JAMIA papers, funding sits here if there is no Funding heading.

**AI-use disclosure.** Required by most journals now. Name each tool, its version, and the task it served ("ChatGPT (GPT-4o) was used to improve spelling and grammar; no content was generated"). Cite the COPE position or the journal policy. Place it in Acknowledgements or in a "Declaration of generative AI use" heading if the journal has one. If an LLM generated figures or code, say so in Methods as well.

**Ethics.** Lives in the first Methods subsection, not in the back matter, except where the journal has a "Data sharing and ethics approval" heading (some Lancet reviews).

**Reporting summary.** Nature-portfolio: "Further information on research design is available in the Nature Portfolio Reporting Summary linked to this article." Other journals: the completed TRIPOD, STARD or PRISMA checklist as a supplementary file, named in Methods.

**Supplementary material statement.** "Supplementary material is available at [journal] online." (EHJ, JAMIA).

**Keywords.** Three to six, after the abstract, for EHJ and JAMIA; MeSH terms where asked.

## House reference format

When the user's manuscript or a reference list is prepared outside a reference manager, use this style: surname then initials with no periods, comma separated, then a period; the full title; then the venue and year. Example shape:

Author A, Author B, Author C. Full title of the article. Journal Name, Year.

For a manuscript under review: "Under review, Year." For a preprint: "medRxiv, Year, DOI."

When the target journal is known, convert to its style at submission (Vancouver numbering for all five families; Nature-portfolio drops article titles for some reference types).
